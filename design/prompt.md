# TabShare flow concept

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. This is a design reference, not a screenshot of the implemented app.

Use case: ui-mockup. Create one high-fidelity product design and user-flow board for TabShare, a simple shared-expense web app inspired by Splitwise's understandable group-expense workflow, with a distinctive original identity. Landscape 1536x1024, crisp legible polished UI, flat front-facing screens, no device photography. Four numbered panels showing a continuous realistic journey: 01 Your groups (large desktop panel, about half the board), 02 Group expenses, 03 Add an expense, 04 Settle up (three smaller mobile-width panels). Design is an everyday useful product, not a landing page: no marketing hero. Calm warm ivory background, deep aubergine typography and primary buttons, soft lilac surfaces, muted sage for money owed to you, restrained terracotta for money you owe. Friendly rounded sans typography, generous spacing, soft 12px corners, fine borders, minimal monochrome icons and initial avatars. Clear persistent navigation and one prominent primary action per screen. Panel 1 brand 'TabShare', title 'Your groups', button '+ New group', active group card 'Coastal weekend' with '3 people', personal label 'You are Alex', net balance 'You get back $240.00', and subtle link 'Try a sample group'. Other group placeholder 'Add your next shared adventure' as empty-state action, not fake live data. Panel 2 'Coastal weekend', tabs 'Expenses' and 'Balances', primary '+ Add expense', total '$720.00', chronological ledger Beach house $480.00 paid by Alex, Saturday dinner $150.00 paid by Jamie, Petrol & parking $90.00 paid by Sam, invite people link in header. Panel 3 compact form 'Add an expense', description 'Saturday dinner', large amount '$150.00', 'Paid by Jamie', 'Split equally' with Alex/Jamie/Sam selected and '$50.00 each', optional details collapsed, button 'Save expense'. Panel 4 'Settle up', 'Jamie pays Alex', '$90.00', explicit text 'Record a payment made outside TabShare', primary 'Record payment', secondary Cancel, small success treatment 'Payment recorded'. Thin directional arrows connect panel headers to show groups > expenses > add > balances/settle. Design should feel refined, warm, focused, original and genuinely usable, avoid blue portfolio styling, gradients, decorative charts, excessive stats, illustrations, and finance jargon. All displayed money must be coherent: Alex paid 480, Jamie150, Sam90, total720 split3; Alex gets240, Jamie owes90, Sam owes150.
